FS — SKU Mix Impact

Tax Specialists handle a significantly higher share of Basic returns (49.3% vs 28.1%) and far fewer Premium returns (18.0% vs 43.3%). This favorable SKU mix is the primary driver of TS's lower overall CST (2.57 vs 2.96), even though per-SKU CST is similar or slightly higher for TS on Basic and Deluxe tiers.

At PL4, Tax Specialists achieve the lowest per-SKU CST across all three tiers (Basic: 1.52 vs 1.81, Deluxe: 1.96 vs 2.49, Premium: 3.16 vs 4.12).
